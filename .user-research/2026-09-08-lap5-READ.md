# Lap 5 · Beat 3 · READ — three records, one person

## 0 · THE ANSWER FIRST

**One of these three is customer evidence. The other two are the builder inspecting his own
product, and they are worth having for a different reason.**

The split does not run *between* the records by author — all three have the same author. It runs
**through** them, by what class of claim each one makes:

| record | what class of claim | can the builder-bias reach it? |
|---|---|---|
| **2 · no weather station** | a **fact about his own household** that the product contradicts | ⛔ **No.** He cannot be wrong about whether he owns a weather station. |
| **3 · should this be a card?** | a **product-design judgement**, self-hedged (*"which I'm not sure it should be"*) | ✅ Entirely. This is the product owner, not the resident. |
| **1 · no left border** | a **craft inspection** | ✅ Entirely. No customer says "no border to the left side of the card." |

⭐ **That table is the whole finding.** Record 2 is the same class of evidence as the 2026-07-26
rainfall event — *a person checking the record against their own world and finding it disagrees* —
which this project already holds as the strongest single piece of user evidence it owns. Records 1
and 3 are good QA and good direction. They are not wants.

## 1 · THE RANKING — inside the customer lane only

### ⭐ 1st · The product asserts a fault about a component the household does not have

> *"I don't need a weather station offline indicator if I don't have a weather station"* — `fb-vurlf77f`

**Evidence**

- `validated` — the report itself: a real person, on his real household, on production, timestamped
  in the store. He is the authority on his own home's hardware, and that is the entire claim.
- `validated` — **it is not one household's problem; it is every household's.** Read directly from
  the instance files 2026-09-08: `fernwood.json` declares `station: "present"`. **`home.json`,
  `qa.json`, `bob.json` and `paul.json` all declare `"declared-absent"`.** So the product's *only*
  station-having household is the one it was originally written for, and **every new household
  arrives in the state this record is about.** n=1 person; n=4-of-5 instances.
- `validated` — **the defect is a CLASS, not a string.** Two live renderings of station state ship
  in the same file and only one honours the declaration:
  - `viewer.html:9652` — honours it: `STATION_DECLARED_ABSENT` → *"No station here — regional readings"*.
  - `viewer.html:16736` — **does not**: `stationLive` (`:16687`) is a pure runtime-liveness boolean,
    so a household with no station renders *"Station offline — pressure & rain from the forecast grid."*
- `inferred` — that second site is **latent, not firing today**: `fishing` is listed in `home.json`'s
  `absent` array, so it does not render at his condo. It fires at the first household that enables
  fishing. Flagging it as a class member, not as today's cause.
- `assumption` (synthetic, three seats, and used only as corroboration that the honest string exists
  and reads well): `mom` — *"'No station here — regional readings' is the right answer and I want it
  kept"*; `wide-eyed` and `owner` found the same string in the QA DOM.

**⛔ AND THE MECHANISM ON THE RECORD MAY BE WRONG.** The DISPOSE ruling states *"the honest string
exists and the wrong one is reaching production."* I could not verify that, and a **second
hypothesis fits the same evidence with a different fix**:

> `home.json` declares `declared-absent`, which routes to the *correct* branch — and that branch
> still renders **`<span class="live-dot stale"></span>`** before the words. It is the right text
> **inside a status-indicator component**. Paul's sentence objects to an *indicator*, not to a
> *string*.

⚠️ Both hypotheses are consistent with every fact I hold. They have different fixes, and building
the recorded one would be waste if the second is true.

**Falsifier — cheap, ~60 seconds, and it should run before anything is scoped:** open the production
household in a browser, expand the weather card, and read the station row's DOM. If the text says
*"Not online"* or *"Station offline"*, the recorded mechanism is right. If it says *"No station here
— regional readings"* **with a dot beside it**, the defect is the indicator affordance, not the copy,
and the fix is in the component, not the string.

**What I would need to ask Paul:** one question — *"when you wrote that, were you reading words that
were wrong, or a light that shouldn't be there?"* He is the only source and it settles it.

> *"The formatting of the What You've Told Me Contents are off. No border to the left side of the

**Evidence**

  `.told-row { padding: 10px 0; border-bottom: 1px solid …; }` — a **bottom** divider, and no left

  under two minutes. That is a repeatable prediction about *new* surfaces, not a fact about this one.

⛔ **Lowest of the three on customer stakes, and I am not hedging that.** No evidence of any kind —
real or synthetic — connects this to anyone failing to do anything. It is correctly routed to a
UX/design theme rather than a one-off fix, and the routing is the valuable part.

**Falsifier:** if the two nearest new-in-2026-09 surfaces also diverge from `.br-`, this is a
process finding about how surfaces get authored and belongs to the design-system theme. If they
conform, this is one card and one CSS block.

## 2 · ⭐ CRITICAL IN MY OWN LANE, AND NOT YET NAMED ANYWHERE

### 2.1 The product's default household is the one Fernwood is not

`validated` (instance files, read today) — 4 of 5 declare `station: "declared-absent"`; 1 declares
`present`, and it is the estate every string in the app was originally written against.

⭐ **The general form, which is bigger than a weather station:** any string derived from Fernwood's
own hardware, phrased as a **fault state** — *offline*, *not online*, a status dot, a gauge that
went quiet — will read at every other household as **"your home is broken."** The household has no
way to tell *the product is misconfigured for me* from *something of mine has failed*, and the
second reading is the one that costs trust. Trust is this project's own named load-bearing emotion.

⚠️ `inferred`, and deliberately not stated harder: I verified two sites and the class is a
generalization from two. It is a **search instruction**, not a census: *grep the fault-state
vocabulary and check each site against `identity.station` and its siblings.*

⛔ **Not mine to scope.** Naming the class is the research contribution; the sweep is engineering's.

### 2.2 Record 2 is a MEASUREMENT OPPORTUNITY this loop is about to spend and not collect

`inferred` — Paul is, at this moment, the **only** person who has ever arrived at this product as a
household with nothing to measure with. Every other household is a fixture. When Mom or Bob arrives,

## 3 · ⭐ IS THERE A REAL CUSTOMER SIGNAL HERE? — the ruling

> ## **This is an operator inspecting his own product, on a real household, and both halves of that sentence are load-bearing.**

**The half that says it is NOT customer signal:**

- `validated` — n=1, and that 1 wrote the code, shipped `card-told` the same day, and holds the
  administrator role. `~/.claude/user-research/fernwood.md § Builder-user structural bias` is
  exactly this shape and is already on the record.
- `validated` — the **posture is not naive at any point.** "No border to the left side of the card"
  and "it should have a jump strip menu" are the vocabulary of someone who knows what a card and a
  jump strip are because he named them. Records 1 and 3 could not have been produced by a customer.
- `validated` (prior lap, §1.1(2), same conclusion independently) — *"Paul-as-customer and
  Paul-as-builder are two people in one record"*, and five of his fifteen lap-2 findings were
  rulings, not wants. **Two of three here are that class.** The ratio held.
- ⛔ Therefore: **these three records may not be described as "what customers want."** A board that
  renders them that way is laundering one administrator's QA notes.

**The half that says it is not NOTHING — and this is the part I would not let get lost:**

- ⭐ `validated` — **the estate is real even though the reader is not naive.** est-e6696a is his
  actual home. It genuinely has no weather station, genuinely is a condo, genuinely has no land.
  **A fact-of-household report is a different class of evidence from a preference report**, and the
  builder-bias objection does not reach it. Record 2 is that class.
- `validated` (precedent, 2026-07-26) — the strongest user evidence this project owns is of exactly
  this shape: a person disbelieved a number because they were standing in the world it described,
  and was right by 14×. **Record 2 is that same instrument pointed at a different fact.**

### ⭐ What this changes for the next beats — the operative part

1. **Record 2 travels as customer evidence.** Tag it `validated`, cite it as *a household reporting
   a mismatch between the product's model and its own facts*, and note that 4 of 5 instances share
   the condition. ⛔ Settle the mechanism (§1, falsifier) **before** scoping, or a build may be aimed
   at the wrong half.
2. **Records 1 and 3 travel as OPERATOR INSPECTION.** Both are already correctly routed to themes
   rather than fixes. They should be **ranked on craft and coherence, not on customer impact** —
   there is no customer impact evidence for either and inventing some would be the failure this
   whole beat exists to prevent.
3. ⛔ **Nothing here closes GAP 1.** Beat 3 has now run twice, in two consecutive laps, on a record
   containing **only the builder**. That is a structural property of this beat worth naming out
   loud: *the READ beat currently reads the product owner.* It is not a defect in the beat — it is
   the accurate state of the evidence — but it means **the beat's exit condition ("what matters most
   to the customer") is being answered from a population of one, and will be until someone else
   walks through the door.**
