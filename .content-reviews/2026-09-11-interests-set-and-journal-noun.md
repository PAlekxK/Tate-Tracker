# QUESTION 1 — the nine labels and the question sentence, as ONE set

## The set rule I am recommending (one sentence, so the next label has a test)

> **Every row names an ACTIVITY in the shortest true words for it, attached to the reader's own
> belongings — and not one row names a building, an acreage or a tenure.**

Two consequences worth stating because both were live tonight:

- **"Gardening" is not an exception to the rule; it satisfies it.** A gerund *is* the activity, and it
  is the shortest true name English has for that one. The 09-10 reframe was right for the six rows
  that were genuinely NOUNS (*Household systems*, *Papers and documents*, *Equipment and tools*,
  *Vehicles*, *Wildlife*, *Handing it all over*) and never applied here. `[paul-ruled]`
- **The presumption lives in the noun, never the verb** — the rule that lets a condo reader meet ten
  of eleven rows without meeting a room they do not have.

### The question sentence — HOLD, verbatim

> **`What would you like to spend time on at your place?`**

- It is Paul's reframe (*"bring it one step closer to the customer by just asking what they'd like to
  do"*) with the two measured fixes kept: **"at your place"** rather than "on here" (which re-opens

  screen of eleven buttons reads as an action menu — *pick one and something happens now*.

### ⭐ One line about "one field, many derived facts" applied to the SET, not to a row

Rows 2 and 10 both build `papers` + `succession` — and the overlap is the point, not a redundancy:
the modules are the same, **the motive is not** (custody now vs. handing it on), and the motive is the
one fact no module list can derive. Conversely nothing here asks for what a tap already yields: one
tap gives us an interest, an ORDER, and a position-vs-preference series. **The ask is one field; the
derived facts are three.** ⚠️ The third is currently underivable — `/api/onboarding-metrics` has no
GET (TIER 2 · 37), so *an event with no reader is not instrumentation*.

## ⛔ FALSIFIER FOR THE SET (what observation shows the set is wrong)

**Secondary, per Paul's own evidence rule.** If Mom re-ranks under these labels and `garden` moves off

# QUESTION 2 — the noun: **Journal**

## Recommendation: **Journal**, and the substitution is already ruled at the vocabulary layer — what is left is the SURFACES

`VOCABULARY.md` §4 `[paul-ruled 2026-09-10]`: *"if journalName is what we're gonna call it, let's just
call it the Journal"* — and the row above it already rejected **"Almanac" as a portable noun**: a
**genre promise** (seasonal, cyclical) earned here by 178 month-keyed season notes, *false at a
gardenless condo*. So Q2 is not "which noun"; it is **"apply the ruled noun where the engine mints a
name, and nowhere else."**

**Three pieces of evidence, none of them mine:**
1. The falsifier arrived on its own: the arithmetic produced **"the condo Almanac"** and **"Home
   Almanac"** — a seasonal promise made to a PO-box household that had been told on the same screen it
   cannot work out a season.
2. **It is already the project's voice** — CLAUDE.md has said *a field journal, not a task manager*
   since the start, and it names an **object in a house**, the same test that seated *the safe*.
3. **It is Paul's own spoken word for it**, tonight, unprompted: *"all input that someone provides to
   their **journal** or feedback…"* · *"every **journal entry** someone makes"* · *"a **query to the
   journal**"* (CYCLE-LOG, the "synced" ruling). He uses it for both halves — the record and the
   asking — which is exactly the load the noun has to carry.

## ⛔ The guard, and it is the load-bearing half

**`journalName` / `journalTile` per instance is untouched, so Fernwood keeps saying *Fernwood
Almanac* — because Fernwood SUPPLIED that word.** *"Each estate names its own thing"* was never
overturned; it now has a word for the thing.

Two places where that guard can leak, both flagged, neither repaired from this seat:

1. **`viewer.html:20014`** is `__HOUSEHOLD_NAME + " Almanac"` — a runtime OVERRIDE of the build-time
   instance value. ⚠️ **Before applying, verify what Mom's live Fernwood renders**: if
   `__HOUSEHOLD_NAME` is set there, a bare substitution renames the make-or-break user's card to
   *Fernwood Journal* with no ribbon behind it. The safe form is **prefer the instance's supplied name;
   mint `<name> Journal` only when none was supplied.**
2. **`tools/build-digest.py:543`** — `journalTile or (name + " Almanac")` is **the fifth site minted by
   arithmetic somewhere else**, which is precisely the return path ux-expert's row 44 warned about.
   It must move with the writer, or *Almanac* walks back in through the digest.

## ⛔ FALSIFIER FOR THE NOUN

**A Fernwood reader asking where the Almanac went** is the falsifier for the *guard* (the instance name
leaked), and it is the one that costs the most — so it is worth probing before it is worth waiting for.

**For the WORD itself:** across the next several founded households, if people's own free text — a
feedback note, a first query, or the rename field on `settings/place/` — reaches for a **different
noun more often than "journal"**, or if most households actually use that rename field, then the
engine default is not the word they have for their record and *Journal* has failed the same way
*Almanac* did. ⚠️ Heavy use of the rename field is **not** a failure on its own — being nameable is the
design; the failure is heavy renaming **away from journal-shaped words**.
