## The four lines that are load-bearing

1. ⭐⭐ **"for [the condo]"** — this is the antecedent the page cannot generate. `s2` asks *"Where is
   your place?"* and the record says she is both owner and resident, so no wording on the page fully
   disambiguates it. The message closes what the page structurally cannot, forty seconds earlier.
2. ⭐⭐ **The sharing line states the real property.** ⛔ Never write *"it won't work for anyone
   else"* — that is false. The link **is** the credential: the gate-1 walker found this unprompted
   (*"if that link got loose, whoever has it walks straight into my address"*). Give the reason
   before the instruction.
3. **"nobody sees it but me"** — the same promise the page makes on `s2`. ⚠️ It becomes a written
   system constraint the day it is sent.
4. **"nothing in there you can get wrong"** — aimed at her documented constraint, and it is a claim
   about the flow rather than praise of her. ⚠️ **It must stay true.** It is true today: the address
   is the only typed field, the confirm has a blameless second option, and everything is optional.
   **Any future step with a wrong answer retires this sentence.**
