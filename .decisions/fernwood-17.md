## fernwood-17 · Does a lens have inputs of its own?

- project: Tate-Tracker
- loop: tate-tracker
- source: `.plans/2026-09-10-testing-architecture-PLAN.md` §6, minted 2026-09-10
- options: reading-posture-only | a-focus-hint-but-never-data | full-inputs

### Why it's here
Convergence between lenses is only evidence if they read **identical artifacts** (plan §2c). If two lenses see different inputs, agreement between them proves nothing.

### What it means
This card exists so the question is **reachable**. The plan it came from is 534 lines and its own
thesis is *"a capability the loop cannot reach by running its own procedure is not a capability the
loop has"* — and it then put eight rulings into prose nothing reads. ⛔ It stalled for an hour on
exactly that the day it landed. The card carries the recommendation and the alternatives so the
ruling can be made without re-reading the plan.

### Recommendation
**NO — reading posture only.** Property/lens *pairings* live in the journey library, not in the lens.

**Alternatives.** **(b)** a "focus" hint (what to attend to) but never data — defensible, and re-opens *"we don't only want to test what's new"*. **(c)** full inputs — ⛔ loses the convergence argument entirely.
