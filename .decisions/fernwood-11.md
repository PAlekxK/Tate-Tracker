### Why it's here
Measured in lap 8's window, and the split is total: **every affordance that asks her to answer us took
zero** — Mama's Perspective queue 0 of 10 offers tapped, the acknowledgment ribbon 0 of 10, the
front-door launcher 0 of 10, the look-for prompt 0 of 5 — while **the jump strip, which only moves
her, took 5 of 5**. In the same window she saved 4 notes, opened the composer 4 times, authored 4 Guru
turns, and handed the project an entire new domain record unprompted (the refrigerator).

⭐ So *"she is not engaging"* was never true; *"she is not answering OUR questions"* is. Those are
different findings with opposite remedies, and this loop spent laps reading the first one. **Depth 2
and depth 3 are both zero** — she reads card faces and does not open individuals — so any remedy that
puts its fix *behind a disclosure* is answering a question she has not asked.

### What it means
- **re-scope-the-confirm-queue** — treat the 0/10 as a verdict on the instrument and rebuild or retire
  it. ⚠️ Premature on this evidence: n is **one active day**, a deviceId is a browser bucket, and BACKLOG
  A1's 2026-07-26 finding already established the confound — her documented fear is being *wrong*, and
  answering is precisely what that blocks. The new data may be re-measuring that, not discovering
  something else.
- **keep-asking-and-instrument-her-doors** — leave the queue alone and prefer learning things from
  channels she opens herself before authoring another ask. Cheapest, reversible, and it is what the new
  CLAUDE.md doctrine ("latch onto what she starts") already directs at leg 1.
- **make-the-guru-the-primary-capture-surface** — follow the behaviour all the way. ⛔ This is a
  **material change to the AI boundary** and Paul's alone: capture is currently deterministic and
  AI-free by rule, and the Guru is on the *ask* path.

### Recommendation
**keep-asking-and-instrument-her-doors**, and convene `user-researcher` first — this is a question
about a person, not a component, which is what the leg-4 seat sequence is for. The measurement is real
and worth acting on; the *inference* from one active day to "the confirm surface is wrong" is not yet
earned. Deciding this by rebuilding her surface would be the reflex the evidence most invites and
least supports.
