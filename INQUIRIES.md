## 2026-06-29 — Water lilies in the pond, yellowing pads

Mom photographed the pond water lilies and asked about yellowing leaves + care tips
(photo IMG_7394, on her phone). Some pads yellowing — mostly the older ones and the
cluster toward one edge, a few with bronzy mottling. Working read: likely routine
older-pad turnover + sun mottling, not a crisis. Trimming the dead pads is safe
regardless.

**Questions to settle before acting:**
1. Are these lilies **potted or rooted in the pond bottom**? Decides whether the
   fertilizer-tab / repotting advice (the common "fix") even applies.
2. **Species** — our ornamental *Nymphaea*, or a native spatterdock / yellow pond-lily
   (*Nuphar*)? Possible yellow flower in the frame. If native, yellowing is even more
   routine and there's essentially nothing to "fix."
3. **Sun exposure** of the pond — a tree is reflected in the shot; partial shade could
   be a factor.

**Context:** This came in alongside an AI answer Mom got (generic but horticulturally
sound) whose one weakness was *assuming the lilies are potted* — exactly the gap a
property-aware assistant would close. Noted as a small signal for the open
"where property-context lives — Garden Guru vs. in-Fernwood Claude" thread.
