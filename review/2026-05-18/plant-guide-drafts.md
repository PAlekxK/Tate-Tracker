# Plant Guide First-Sentence Anchoring — Drafts

**Date:** 2026-05-18
**Charter applied:** `~/.claude/content-principles/tate-tracker.md` — *"Anchor the first sentence; let the rest be encyclopedic if it must."*
**Scope:** First sentence only for each plant's `guide` field. Rest of prose stays as-is.
**Convention:** Open with *"the [plant] on the property"* / *"the [plants] here"* / *"the [plant] by [feature]"* / *"our [plants]"*. Avoid anthropomorphizing verbs ("thrives," "loves," "enjoys") in favor of "is at home," "does well," "wants," "sits."

---

### white-pine — Eastern White Pine

**Current first sentence:** "Eastern white pines thrive in Jasper's climate — they prefer well-drained acidic soil and full sun to partial shade."

**Proposed first sentence:** "The white pines on the property are at home in this climate — acidic mountain soil, full sun, drought-tolerant once established."

**Reasoning:** Charter exemplar; matches the worked example. Anchors in *this* place's soil and aspect rather than "Jasper's climate" (generic).
