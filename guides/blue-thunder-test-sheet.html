Meter settings

T1–T4 — DC VOLTS. 20 V DC or auto. Red in VΩ, black in COM. Straight across the battery posts.
T5 — DC AMPS. Red moves to the 10 A jack, meter in series: neg post → meter → neg cable. Never across the posts.
ORDER MATTERS. DO NOT SHUT IT OFF BETWEEN T2 AND T3.
A short idle run on this bike is a net withdrawal — headlight always on, 150 W only at 5 000 r/min. Start it once and stay running through T3. Charger fully off the battery (not just unplugged), rested 1 hr minimum before T1.
