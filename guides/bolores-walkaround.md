# Bolores — Walk-Around Punch List

*1989 Ford Bronco Eddie Bauer · capture sheet for the full restoration scope*

Walk the truck once, slowly, and write down **everything** — even small stuff. The point is to surface every fix *before* the teardown so jobs converge into the paint window instead of becoming separate teardowns later. Anything found here that's worth tracking gets promoted into the `bronco-1989` restoration list in `vehicles.json`.

**Phase tagging** (so each find lands in the right place in the sequence — see `guides/bolores-door-panel-repair.md`):
- **P0** — independent mechanical, do anytime
- **P1** — bodywork to scope/repair *before* primer
- **P2** — anything done while panels/trim are off (the big teardown)
- **P3** — handled at the paint booth
- **P4** — reassembly (deadening, headliner, trim back on)
- **P5** — audio, last

For each item note: **what · where · how bad · phase · who (Paul / Larry / shop) · open question**.
