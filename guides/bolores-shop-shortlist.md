## The framing: it's three trades, not one

A true one-stop full-restoration shop exists, but it's a **different project** — those shops bill
$100+/hr and would swallow the whole build, trading the DIY-forward model (and Larry) for a
five-figure check. What's realistic is **handing off whole trades**:

| Trade | Who | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Body + paint | **Larry** (prep/prime) + his buddy's shop (spray) | ✅ settled |
| Teardown, panel repair, sound-deadening, reassembly | **Paul** | ✅ settled, and the fun part |
| **Auto upholstery / trim** (carpet, both headliner panels, seat leather) | *open* → see below | 🎯 the obvious handoff |
| **Classic mechanical / electrical** (lock circuit, cruise vacuum) | *open* → see below | optional handoff |

The split is already right. The shortlist below only fills the two open rows.

## Questions to bring to Larry

These gate real sequencing decisions elsewhere in the plan:

1. **Are the doors going to bare metal, or scuffed and sprayed?**
   → decides whether the door-cavity clean/treat/deaden happens *before* or *after* paint. If any
   stripping, heat, filler or metalwork touches a door skin, cavity work waits (and if they end up
   cutting a door, anything done inside it is gone).
2. **Is the front floor pan (under the carpet) in scope?** And if not — **can you shoot it while the
   interior's already stripped?** Cheap add, and it hands back cured metal to deaden onto, which
   settles the "who paints the pan" question and the cure gate in one move.
3. **Bed in body-color Cabernet** — confirm, and what finish holds up to being walked on.
4. **Interior plastic panels in chestnut** — panels sprayed off the truck, separately?
5. **Roughly how long is she with you?** (Sizes the panel-off window — see the one-removal protocol
   in `guides/bolores-door-panel-repair.md`.)
