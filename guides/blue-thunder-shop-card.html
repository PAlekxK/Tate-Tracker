If Blue Thunder won’t start

2017 Suzuki DR200S · shop card · 2026-08-30

Before you press the button — 20 seconds

  1. Kill switch on RUN — the red switch, right handlebar.
  2. Petcock on ON — not RES. Save RES for when ON starves.
  3. Look in the tank. She has no fuel gauge. Reserve is only 0.7 gal.
  4. Cold? Choke fully ON, throttle closed.
  5. Read the voltmeter if one is fitted — write the number down.
THREE TRIES. FIVE SECONDS EACH. THEN STOP.
Suzuki’s own limit is 5 seconds of starter at a time — longer overheats the starter motor and the wiring. Wait 10–15 seconds between tries. A bike that is going to start usually starts on the first or second. Every try past the third flattens the battery and erases the evidence of what was actually wrong.
On the FIRST labored crank — before it gets worse — KICK IT. She has a kickstarter as well as the electric start.
  • Fires on the kick → engine, fuel and spark are all fine. It was purely the battery.
  • Won’t fire after several good kicks → not the battery. Fuel or spark.

Write down which one it was

1Nothing at all, or a single click☐
2Starter spins slowly and labors☐
3Starter spins briskly but it never fires☐
4Fires, then dies☐

Then stop and call Paul. Do not keep cranking.

Also worth doing every time: petcock OFF whenever she is parked, and park her level — she seeps a little fuel when she sits at a hard lean.
