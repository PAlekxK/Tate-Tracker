# Recent updates

What's changed at Fernwood lately. Newest first.

---

## 2026-05-28 — The fairway, turf, and a meadow

- The mowed fairway and the meadow beyond it are now their own entries in the Plants card — each with a season-by-season care calendar, the same treatment the seventeen plants get. Turf-type tall fescue for the lush turf; native bunchgrasses and wildflowers for the meadow.
- A new "🚜 Mow" rhythm joins the calendar, so the fairway turns up under This Month when it's time to cut, overseed, lime, or watch for summer brown patch.
- "Worth considering" gained a "Lawn & turf" shelf and five native meadow grasses — little bluestem, big bluestem, indiangrass, switchgrass, and purpletop — alongside where to source the seed and what to read about the natural approach.
- A small fix on the dashboard's Wildlife tile: "Leaving this week" now sits with the birds instead of looking like it belonged to the lake.

---

## 2026-05-26 — Worth considering: plants for the property

- A new card near the bottom of the dashboard — a short, curated list of native and ecologically-meaningful plants worth bringing to Fernwood, from the American chestnut and white oak to flame azalea and cardinal flower.
- Each entry says why it fits this slope, which natural community it belongs to, and where to find it — with a freshness dot on every source so you can tell how current it is.
- Mom's pick, autumn bentgrass, is in there, flagged as hers.
- Time-sensitive native plant sales are called out right on the entries that point to them.

## 2026-05-21 — Photos for Garden Guru

- The 📷 button next to the unified input now takes photos. Snap a plant or animal at the property and Garden Guru will identify it.
- Two confirmations before anything lands in the canon: *"Does that look right?"* then *"Worth adding to the Almanac?"* — a quiet beat to think twice before the journal grows.
- A Yes to both kicks off the work — Garden Guru drafts the schema entry, commits it to the repo, and the new species shows up in the right tab on the next dashboard refresh (about a minute or three).
- The unified input collapsed to a single button. Text-only submissions still save quietly without a model call; photos route to Garden Guru.

## 2026-05-21 — Surface polish, mammal media, audit trim

- All 17 mammals now have photos (Wikimedia Commons, CC-licensed) — the mammal tab no longer feels half-built.
- Five mammals gained reference audio for their distinctive vocalizations: coyote, red fox, black bear, raccoon, white-tailed deer.
- Salamanders explicitly note "silent" rather than appearing as missing-sound gaps.
- The unified input's "Note or ask the slope" label is gone, replaced by a small helper line: "Record an observation, ask a question, or suggest an improvement."

- Fernwood card subtitle expanded — "Elevation · Microclimate · Soils" → "Land · Sky · History" — to reflect the card's broader scope (Cherokee history, marble heritage, watershed, sky quality).
- Behind the scenes: CLAUDE.md trimmed from 495 lines to ~205. Archived sections moved to `.history/`.
